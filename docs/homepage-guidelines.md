# Homepage Implementation Guidelines

This document outlines how the Design System is applied to the specific sections of the Homepage.

## Hero (Fullscreen)
- **Container:** Full bleed width, `h-[calc(100vh-104px)]`
- **Typography:** Hero Title (48px+)
- **Buttons:** Hero Button (rounded-full)
- **Background:** Gradient (from-[#f3f4f8] to-[#c7eef0])

## About
- **Section Spacing:** Medium Section (py-20)
- **Typography:** Section Title (36px), Section Badge
- **Buttons:** Primary Button (rounded-xl)
- **Cards:** Card Small (p-6, rounded-2xl)
- **Background:** White, with Green-50 decorative band

## Services
- **Section Spacing:** Medium Section (py-20)
- **Typography:** Section Title (36px)
- **Cards:** Card Medium (p-8, rounded-2xl)
- **Background:** Slate-50

## Why Us
- **Section Spacing:** Medium Section (py-20)
- **Typography:** Section Title (36px)
- **Buttons:** Primary Button (rounded-xl)
- **Background:** White

## Testimonials
- **Section Spacing:** Medium Section (py-20)
- **Typography:** Section Title (36px)
- **Cards:** Card Small (rounded-2xl)
- **Background:** White

## FAQ
- **Section Spacing:** Small Section (py-16)
- **Typography:** Section Title (36px)

## CTA / Footer CTA
