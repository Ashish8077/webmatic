# About Page Implementation Guidelines

This document outlines how the Design System is applied to the specific sections of the About Us page.

## Hero
- **Container:** Standard Banner, `h-[600px]`
- **Typography:** Hero Title (48px+), Section Badge
- **Buttons:** Hero Button (rounded-full)
- **Background:** Image with dark navy overlay gradient

## Statistics
- **Section Spacing:** Medium Section (py-20)
- **Typography:** Large Feature Title (72px for numbers), Body (18px for titles)
- **Cards:** Card Medium (p-8, rounded-3xl)
- **Background:** White

## Company Overview
- **Section Spacing:** Large Section (py-24)
- **Typography:** Section Title (40px+), Dropcap (54px), Section Badge
- **Buttons:** Hero Button (rounded-full)
- **Cards:** Card Large (p-10+, rounded-3xl)
- **Background:** White

## Mission & Vision
- **Section Spacing:** Large Section (py-24)
- **Typography:** Section Title (32px)
- **Cards:** Card Large (p-10+, rounded-3xl)
- **Background:** White

## Core Values
- **Section Spacing:** Large Section (py-24)
- **Typography:** Section Title (40px+), Section Badge
- **Cards:** Card Large (p-10, rounded-3xl)
- **Background:** Slate-50

## Team Members
- **Section Spacing:** Large Section (py-24)
- **Typography:** Section Title (40px+), Section Badge
- **Cards:** Card Medium (p-8, rounded-3xl image cards)
- **Background:** Slate-50
